This is the one page to read before the other 20. It states what's already built and costed, what the enterprise-scale plan looks like, what it costs, and exactly what the Board is being asked to approve today — with a link to the full proof behind every claim.

Decisions Requested Today

  • Approve the Enterprise Agentic AI & AI Agent Governance Policy (v2.0) Establishes risk tiers, autonomy limits, non-negotiable restrictions, and Board/committee accountability before any further scale-up — see AI-GPolicy
  • Charter the Board AI & Technology Committee and formally appoint a Chief AI Officer Names the accountable executive and oversight body — see Governance Structure §6 and AI Roadmap → Governance & Operating Model
  • Approve the Go-Live Validation Gates as the pass/fail standard for all 3 pilots before production launch Concrete, measurable thresholds — not yet-measured results — see Governance → Validation Gates
  • Approve Wave 2 scoping (Retail Lending, AML/Transaction Monitoring, Contact-Centre AI, Treasury) to begin at Month 6 Candidate list and sequencing rationale — see AI Roadmap → Use-Case Portfolio
Why This Is Safe to Approve
No agent holds unilateral authority over money movement, a fraud confirmation, or a lending decision.

Every one of the 3 pilots is a draft-and-recommend design — a human credit officer, fraud analyst, or collections officer signs off on every consequential action. See Governance → Human-in-the-Loop.

Why This Isn't Overselling
None of the 3 pilots have run in production yet — so we're not showing you a measured result we don't have.

Instead of an invented accuracy number, the Board is asked to approve concrete, measurable go/no-go thresholds each pilot must clear before launch. See Governance → Validation Gates.
